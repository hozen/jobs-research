一句话核心

利润不是算出来的，是分类出来的。同一笔支出，是当期费用还是多年受益的资产，决定了它进利润表还是进资产负债表；而「这笔支出该归谁管」的判断口径，就是会计里最值钱、也最容易被动手脚的地方。

本讲导读Session guide

利润表是三张报表里最容易被误读的一张：数字层层相减，每一层的口径都不一样，毛利高不等于净利高，营业利润高也可能被利息吃掉。 这一讲先把「利润是怎么量出来的」讲清楚——用权责发生制（accrual）还是收付实现制、费用怎么分类（营业、筹资、资本性）、每一层利润分别扣掉了什么；再讲收入的确认时点与拆分披露，以及那些让利润表「不干净」的东西：非经常性损益、pro-forma 备考数据、来自非经营性投资的收益。Damodaran 的态度很直白：pro-forma 数据永远不要照单全收，投资人得自己设计一套「嗅觉测试」，判断哪些费用该加回来。 最后回到第 4 页那张图：「万物皆有其位」——一笔支出的归宿取决于它能带来几年收益，资产负债表的资产端与利润表的费用行，靠折旧这条线连起来。

幻灯 2计量收入：权责发生制（accrual）与收付实现制Measuring Income: Accrual versus Cash Accounting

在权责发生制会计（accrual accounting）下，你在交易发生时就记录，而不是等现金进出时才记录。

  • 收入在产品或服务售出时确认，不是等客户付款时才确认。
  • 费用一致地确认：与生产已售出产品或服务相关的费用，计入它所属的那一期——哪怕这笔钱你上个月已经付了，或者要到下个月才付。

在收付实现制（cash accounting）下，你收到钱才记收入，付钱才记费用。

除非你做的是小本生意或个人生意，否则你必须遵守权责发生制的规则。

English originalMeasuring Income: Accrual versus Cash Accounting q In accrual accounting, you record transactions when they occur, rather than when cash flows occur. q Revenues are recorded when a product or service is sold, not when the customer pays for that product or service. q Expenses are recorded consistently, with the expenses associated with producing the sold product or service shown in the period, even though you may have spent the money in a prior period or will not pay until a future period. q In cash accounting, you record revenues when you get paid for providing a product or service, and expenses when you pay. q Unless you are a small or personal business, you will have to follow accrual accounting rules.

幻灯 3费用分类：营业费用、筹资费用与资本性支出Classifying Expenses: Operating, Financing and Capital Expenses

营业费用（operating expenses）：与企业经营相关的费用。既包括生产所售产品或服务的直接成本，也包括与生产相关的其他费用，比如销售、一般及行政费用（SG&A）。

筹资费用（financing expenses）：与使用非权益资本有关的费用。最常见的形态就是债务上的利息支出（interest expense）。

资本性支出（capital expenses）：能带来多年收益的支出。对制造业公司来说，通常是厂房与设备；对非制造业公司来说，形态可能更不传统、更无形——而会计向来不擅长处理这类东西。

English originalClassifying Expenses: Operating, Financing and Capital Expenses ¨ Operating expenses are expenses associated with the operations of the business. That includes not only the direct costs of producing the product or service the firm sells, but also other expenses associated with production, including S, G & A expenses. ¨ Financing expenses are expenses associated with the use of non-equity financing. Most often, this takes the form of interest expenses on debt. ¨ Capital expenses are expenses that provide benefits over many years. For a manufacturing company, these can take the form of plant and equipment. For non-manufacturing companies, they can take on less conventional and tangible forms (and accounting has never been good at dealing with these).

幻灯 4万物皆有其位……Everything has a place….

这是整讲的骨架图：利润表（income statement）与资产负债表（balance sheet）在图中间咬合，回答「一笔支出最终去了哪」。

  • 只对本期有收益的支出（Operating expenses）→ 直接进利润表的 Operating Expenses。
  • 能带来多年收益的支出（Capital expenses）→ 先作为资产留在资产负债表上（Shows up as asset on balance sheet），随后在资产寿命期内通过折旧逐期转入利润表（Written off as depreciation over life of asset）。
  • 借款（Borrowings on balance sheet）→ 留在资产负债表的负债端，以利息的形式（Financing expenses）从利润表里扣掉。

换句话说：一笔支出算费用还是算资产，取决于它能管几年；资产负债表的资产端与利润表的费用行，靠折旧这条线连起来。

幻灯 5重看利润表：逐行拆解Revisiting the Income Statement

这一页把利润表摊开来看，每一步都是「减去一项费用，得到一个口径的利润」。

  • 起点 Revenues 收入：会计对本期内企业各项交易产生的收入/销售的估计。
  • 减 Cost of Goods Sold 营业成本：与生产产品或服务相关、对应 top line sales 的费用。
  • 得 Gross Profit 毛利：生产环节的盈利能力。
  • 减 Other Operating Expenses 其他营业费用：销售、一般及行政费用，以及与经营相关但不直接对应产品与服务生产的其他费用。
  • 得 Operating Profit 营业利润：企业整体的盈利能力。
  • 减 Financial Expenses 财务费用：与非权益资本（主要是债务）的使用相关的费用。
  • 得 Taxable Income 应税所得：税前归属于股权投资者的收入。
  • 减 Taxes 所得税：应税所得上应付的税款。
  • 得 Net Income 净利润：税后归属于股权投资者的收入。

幻灯 6收入确认（revenue recognition）Revenue Recognition

对多数公司来说，收入确认是个简单过程：产品或服务一卖出去，就记为收入。

但对另一些公司——尤其是那些要卖很多年产品或服务的公司——这件事就变得棘手，因为多少收入记在销售当年、多少记在以后的年份变成了一个有争议的问题。

在 ASC 606 下：新模型在收入确认上的核心原则是「以反映主体预期有权就这些商品或服务换取的对价金额，描绘向客户转让已承诺商品或服务的情况」。

  • 因此，对一个做多年期开发的房地产开发商来说，收入应当随工程进度确认。
  • 对一家签多年合同的软件公司来说，履约义务（performance obligations）将决定收入在何时确认。
English originalRevenue Recognition ¨ For many firms, revenue recognition is a simple process, where once a product or service is sold, it is recorded as revenues. ¨ For some firms, especially those that sell products or services over many years, it becomes trickier, since the question of how much of the revenue to record in the year of the sale and how much in subsequent years becomes debatable. ¤ Under ASC 606: “The new model’s core principle for revenue recognition is to “depict the transfer of promised goods or services to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration to which the entity expects to be entitled in exchange for those goods or services.” ¤ Thus, for a real estate developer working on a multi-year construction, revenues should be recognized as construction progresses, and for a software firm that enters in a contract over many years, performance obligations will determine when revenues get recognized.

幻灯 7收入拆分（revenue breakdowns）Revenue Breakdowns

当一家公司进入多个业务、多个地区时，知道收入究竟从哪里来就变得有用了。

这种拆分有时会直接出现在利润表里，但更常见的是放在财务报表附注（footnotes）中：

  • 公司一般按地区（geography）拆分收入，不过细度可能不同。
  • 公司也会按业务分部（business segment）拆分收入，不过分部的划分方式本身带有主观性。
English originalRevenue Breakdowns ¨ As companies enter multiple businesses and different geographies, it is useful to know where they generate their revenues. ¨ While the breakdown can sometimes by provided in income statements, they are more likely to be part of the footnotes to the financial statements: ¤ Companies generally break down revenues by geography, though the degree of detail can vary. ¤ Companies also break down revenues by business segment, though there is an element of subjectivity to the segment categorization.

幻灯 8营业费用：拆开看Operating Expenses: A Break Down

营业成本 vs 其他营业费用：营业费用大体分成两块——一块是与生产商品或服务（收入的来源）直接相关的费用，即营业成本（cost of goods sold）；另一块是与经营相关、但与收入没有这么直接关系的费用。

  • 前者在收入端直接扣减，得到毛利（gross profit）。
  • 后者从毛利里扣减，得到营业利润（operating income）。

销售、一般及行政费用（SG&A）：在很多公司里，这是最大的非营业费用项——一个边界模糊的大杂烩，众所周知除了洗碗机本身，什么都往里装。

English originalOperating Expenses: A Break Down ¨ COGS versus Other Operating Expenses: Operating expenses are broadly broken down into expenses directly related to producing the goods or services that give rise to revenues, i.e. cost of goods sold, and expenses that are related to operations, but which are not as directly tied to revenues. ¤ The former are netted out from revenues to get to gross profits ¤ The latter get netted out of gross profits to get to operating income ¨ Selling, General and Administrative Costs: In many companies, the largest non-operating expense is S,G &A, an amorphous item which can include everything but the proverbial kitchen sink.

幻灯 9折旧：会计口径、税务口径与经济口径Depreciation: Accounting, Tax and Economic Forms

同一样资产，有三套折旧口径。

经济折旧（economic depreciation）：反映一项资产随着变老而发生的价值（盈利能力）损失。它需要细致的判断；即使是同一类资产，也会因使用方式不同而有差异。

会计折旧（accounting depreciation）：更机械一些，主要由资产的老化驱动；差别通常在于它是在资产寿命期内均匀摊销，还是加速摊销。

税务折旧（tax depreciation）：反映税务机关在计算应税所得时允许扣除的折旧额。

English originalDepreciation: Accounting, Tax and Economic Forms • Economic depreciation reflects the loss in value (earning power) in an asset, as it ages. It requires nuance, and will vary across even the same type of assets, depending on how it is used. • Accounting depreciation is more mechanical and is driven largely by the aging of the asset, with the differences often being in whether it happens uniformly over the life of the asset or is more accelerated. • Tax depreciation reflects what the tax authorities will allow as depreciation for purposes of computing taxable income.

幻灯 10财务费用（financial expenses）Financial Expenses

最常见的财务费用是债务的利息支出——形式要么是银行贷款，要么是公司债（corporate bonds）。

由于会计把其他承诺（例如租赁）也当作债务来分类，其中一部分利息支出是隐含的：会计根据自己对承诺的债务等价值（debt equivalent value）的评估，再结合当前利率倒算出来。

有些公司会把利息支出与公司从现金持有和金融投资中赚到的利息收入对冲，列报为净利息费用（net interest expense）；如果利息收入超过利息支出，这一行就变成净利息收入。

English originalFinancial Expenses ¨ The most common financial expense is interest expense on debt, either in the form of bank loans or corporate bonds. ¨ As accountants classify other commitments (such as leases) as debt, some of the interest expense is implicit, i.e., it is calculated by accountants based upon their assessment of the debt equivalent value of commitments and current interest rates. ¨ In some companies, interest expenses are netted out against interest income earned by the company on its cash holdings and financial investments, and reported as a net interest expense. If interest income exceeds interest expense, this number will measure net interest income.

幻灯 11非经营性投资的收益Income from non-operating investments

现金与有价证券（Cash & Marketable Securities）：现金持有所赚取的收益（多数公司会把现金投向国库券、商业票据等有价证券），要么单独列报为一项收入，要么与利息支出对冲。

持有其他公司的股份：列报方式取决于持股比例的大小：

  • 当你持有另一家公司的（小比例或少数）股权时，该项投资的收益通常计入利润表。
  • 但如果你持有的是多数股权，一般就必须合并报表（consolidate）——这意味着你要把子公司的收入、营业费用和营业利润 100% 全部算作自己的。
English originalIncome from non-operating investments ¨ Cash & Marketable Securities: Income earned on cash holdings (which is invested in marketable securities, like treasury bills and commercial paper in most companies) will be reported either as a stand alone income or netted against interest expenses. ¨ Cross holdings in other companies: The reporting can vary depending upon the magnitude of your holding: ¤ When you hold a (small or minority) portion of another company, the income from that holding will usually be reported in the income statement. ¤ However, if you hold a majority stake of another company, you will generally have to consolidate your financials. That will require you to count 100% of the subsidiary’s revenues, operating expenses and operating income as your own.

幻灯 12非经常性损益（extraordinary income/expenses）Extraordinary Income/ Expenses

正如这个词的字面意思，非经常性收入与费用是用来捕捉企业在正常经营过程中不会遇到的事项。

非经常性项目包括：

  • 出售资产或业务部门所带来的一次性费用或收益
  • 与过去的某个项目、诉讼或罚款相关的核销（write-off）或计提（charge）
  • 过去收购形成的商誉减值（impairment of goodwill）

如果一项项目确实是非经常性的，它就该很少出现，而且与之相关的金额每次都应当不同。如果它年年都出现，那它就不是非经常性的——哪怕它正负号交替（有的年份是利润、有的年份是亏损）也一样。

English originalExtraordinary Income/ Expenses ¨ As the term implies, extraordinary income and expenses are designed to capture what a company does not face in the ordinary course of operations. ¨ Extraordinary items include ¤ One-time expense or gain from sale of assets or divisions ¤ Write offs or charges associated with past project, lawsuits or fines ¤ Impairment of goodwill from acquisitions in the past ¨ If an item is truly extraordinary, it should show up infrequently and the amount associated with it should vary. If it shows up every year, it is not extraordinary, even if switches signs (goes from profits in some years to losses in others).

幻灯 13备考（Pro-forma）会计：障眼法还是正当的重述？Pro-forma Accounting: Con game or legitimate restatement?

近年来，公司在列报备考财务数据（pro-forma financial statements）时越来越有创意。

  • 有些情况是为了修正他们所认为的会计不一致（accounting inconsistencies）。
  • 另一些情况，动机则是提高自己的盈利能力。

作为投资者，你永远不该照单全收 pro-forma 财务数据，而要自己设计一套「嗅觉测试」（smell test）：判断哪些东西该加回来、哪些不该，才能算出备考利润。

一般而言，有两类项目最值得你盯住：

  • 费用从营业费用挪成资本性支出——有时是正当的，有时不是。
  • 因为「一次性」或「非经常性」而被剔除的费用。
English originalPro-forma Accounting: Con game or legitimate restatement? ¨ In recent years, companies have become creative in reporting pro-forma financial statements. ¤ In some cases, they do so to correct for what they believe are accounting inconsistencies. ¤ In other cases, they are motivated by the desire to increase their profitability. ¨ As investors, you should never take pro-forma financials at face value but devise your own smell test on what should be added back and what should not to get to proforma income. In general, there are two items you should focus the most attention to: ¤ The movement of expenses from operating to capital, sometimes merited, sometimes not. ¤ The removal of expenses because they are one time or extraordinary.

第 1 题

在权责发生制（accrual accounting）下，你在交易发生时记录交易，而不是在收到或付出款项时才记录。假设你在某个财年年初卖出一份 3 年期服务合同，金额 $150 million，其中一半预付，另一半两年后才支付。按照权责发生制的原则，今年你应当列报多少收入？

In accrual accounting, you record transactions as they occur, not when payment is received or made. Assume that you sell a 3-year service contract for $150 million at the start of a fiscal year, and that you get paid half of that amount up front and the other half two years from now. Using accrual accounting principles, how much should you show as revenues this year?

  • a. 5000 万美元 $ 50 million
  • b. 7500 万美元 $ 75 million
  • c. 1.25 亿美元 $ 125 million
  • d. 1.5 亿美元 $ 150 million
  • e. 以上都不对 None of the above
答案：a 5000 万美元（$50 million）

这是一份 3 年期合同，服务要在三年内交付，所以本年只应确认这笔交易收入的三分之一。收款节奏是无关紧要的（至少对利润表而言）。

English originala. $50 million. Since it is a 3-year contract and the services will be spread over three years, I would record one third of the transaction revenues this year. The payment schedule is irrelevant (at least for income statements)

第 2 题

会计在计算毛利和营业利润时，会区分营业成本（cost of goods sold）与其他营业费用。作为一家制造企业，下列各项费用分别应归入这两组中的哪一组？

Accountants draw a distinction between cost of goods sold and other operating expenses in computing gross and operating income. As a manufacturing company, in which of these two groups would you include each of the following expenses?

  • a. 生产产品/服务所用的材料 Material used to produce products/services
  • b. 人工费用 Labor expenses
  • c. 销售与市场费用 Selling and Marketing Expenses
  • d. 一般及行政费用（G&A） G&A costs
  • e. 工厂维护费用 Factory maintenance costs
答案：a, b and e a、b、e 属于营业成本（cost of goods sold）；c、d 属于其他营业费用。

a、b、e 是营业成本，c 与 d 是其他营业费用。工厂维护、人工和材料都与已售出的产品直接相关，销售费用和一般及行政费用则不是。（工厂维护也许不是变动成本，但它仍然是营业成本的一部分。）

English originala, b and e are cost of goods sold, and c & d are other operating expenses. Factory maintenance, labor and material are all related directly to products that are sold. Selling and G&A expenses are not. (Factory maintenance may not be a variable cost, but it is still part of cost of goods sold).

第 3 题

利润表里通常有三个利润口径：毛利（gross profits）、营业利润（operating income）和净利润（net income）。对于一家营业成本极低、销售与一般及行政费用极高、同时债务繁重的公司，你在利润表上会预期看到什么？

There are usually three measures of income in an income statement: gross profits, operating income and net income. For a company that has a very low cost of goods sold, very high selling and G&A expenses and lots of debt, which of the following would you expect to see in the income statement?

  • a. 高毛利、高营业利润、高净利润 High gross profits, high operating profits, high net profits
  • b. 高毛利、低营业利润、高净利润 High gross profits, low operating profits, high net profits
  • c. 高毛利、低营业利润、更低的净利润 High gross profits, low operating profits, even lower net profits
  • d. 低毛利、低营业利润、更低的净利润 Low gross profits, low operating profits, even lower net profits
  • e. 高毛利、高营业利润、低净利润 High gross profits, high operating profits, low net profits
答案：c 高毛利、低营业利润、更低的净利润。

低营业成本应当带来高毛利；但高销售费用和高一般及行政费用会让营业利润变低，而债务上的高利息支出会让净利润更低。

English originalc. High gross profits, low operating profits, even lower net profits. Low cost of goods sold should translate into high gross profits, but high selling and G&A expenses will make operating profit low, and high interest payments on debt will make net profit even lower.

第 4 题

如果你的公司持有另一家公司（子公司）5% 的（少数）股权，这项持股的利润影响预计会出现在利润表的哪里？

If your company owns a 5% (minority) stake in another company (subsidiary), where in the income statement would you expect to see the profit effects of this holding?

  • a. 公司的收入（revenues） In the company’s revenues
  • b. 公司的毛利（gross profits） In the company’s gross profits
  • c. 公司的营业利润（operating profits） In the company’s operating profits
  • d. 公司的净利润（net profits） In the company’s net profits
  • e. 哪儿都不会出现 No where
答案：d 公司的净利润。

持有另一家公司 5% 的股权时，这项持股带来的利润会出现在利润表营业利润线以下，因此是净利润的一部分。

English originald. In the company’s net profits. When you own a 5% stake in another company, the profits from this holding will show up in the income statement below the operating income line and thus will be part of net income.

第 5 题

如果某一项是非经常性费用（extraordinary expense），那么在你的利润表上，随着时间推移，你应当预期这一项呈现什么样的特征？

If an item is an extraordinary expense, which of the following should you expect to see for this item in your income statements over time?

  • a. 该费用每年都会出现，只是金额不同 The expense will show up every year, albeit with different values
  • b. 该费用在多数年份出现，有时为正、有时为负 The expense will show up most years, sometimes positive, sometimes negative.
  • c. 该费用按固定间隔出现（比如每 3 年一次） The expense will show up at regular intervals (every 3 years)
  • d. 该费用出现频率低，且不规律 The expense will show up infrequently and without regularity
  • e. 以上都不对 None of the above
答案：d 该费用出现频率低，且不规律。

如果一项费用每年都出现，或者按固定间隔出现，它就是公司日常经营的一部分，不应被视为非经常性项目。

English originald. The expense will show up infrequently and be irregular. If an expense shows up every year or at regular intervals, it is part of the company’s business operations and should not be considered extraordinary.
译者注
术语说明：本讲保留 gross profit / operating profit / net income / accrual 等会计术语的英文，必要处给出中英对照。 第 5 页表格中 Financial Expenses（财务费用）一行的英文解释与上一行 Cost of Goods Sold 完全相同（原文如此，第 4 页的图上也是同样的重复），属课件笔误；按第 3 页的定义，它应指与非权益融资（主要是债务利息）相关的费用，译者照录原文并在页内注明。 第 13 页标题原文为「Con game or legitimate restatement?」，con game 意为障眼法、把戏（与「骗局 / con」一词双关）。 课后答案文件原文把第 5 题的答案误编号为「6.」，此处按题目顺序对应到第 5 题。
