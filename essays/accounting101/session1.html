一句话核心

会计不是预测未来的工具，也不是估值工具——它是一本已经发生的事的账本。想做估值的人不必懂准则细节，但必须知道报表是怎么被拼出来的，否则你读到的数字就只是数字。

本讲导读Session guide

Damodaran 给这门课开的第一刀就切在会计本身：会计人不预测未来，也不给资产估值，他是记录已经发生的事的历史学家。理解这一点，才能理解他后面为什么敢把准则、折旧、研发资本化这些事「按自己的视角重写一遍」。这一讲先建立三张报表的骨架：资产负债回答「你有什么、你欠什么」，利润表回答「你赚了多少」，现金流量表回答「现金进出了多少」，以及三者之间如何勾稽。

幻灯 2会计人的角色The Accountant's Role

在我看来（很多会计专业人士会不同意），会计的职责是：

  • 核查已经发生的交易与经营行为
  • 以一致的方式把它们记录下来
  • 以标准化的形式报告结果

会计当然想让自己对企业更相关、更居核心，但下面这些不是会计的职责：

  • 预测未来——不管那有多诱人
  • 给资产或经营业务估值

说得直白些：会计人是一位历史学家，负责编撰已经发生的事件，而不是预测未来。

English originalThe Accountant’s Role o In my view (and many accountants will disagree), it is the role of accounting o To check transactions and operations, as they occur o To record them in a consistent manner o To report the results in standardized form o Much as accounting wants to makes itself more relevant and central to businesses, it is not the role of accounting: o Forecast the future, no matter how tempted. o Value assets or operations. o Bluntly put, an accountant is a historian, chronicling events that have already occurred, not predicting the future.

幻灯 3会计要回答的三个问题The Accounting Questions..

  • <strong>你拥有什么？</strong>列出企业投入的资产、为此花了多少钱，以及这些资产今天可能值多少。
  • <strong>你欠着什么？</strong>列明企业为了维持经营必须履约的合同承诺——简单说应当包括所有借款，但并不限于借款。
  • <strong>你赚了多少钱？</strong>衡量企业的盈利能力：既要看会计对费用的判断，也要看现金的流入与流出。
English originalThe Accounting Questions.. ¨ What do you own? List out the assets that a business has invested in, and how much it spent on those investments and perhaps what these assets are worth today. ¨ What do you owe? Specify the contractual commitments that a business has to meet, to stay in business. Simply put, this should include all borrowings, but is not restricted to those. ¨ How much money did you make? Measure the profitability of the business, both with accounting judgments on expenses, and based upon cash in and cash out.

幻灯 4三张报表The Accounting Statements

资产负债表（balance sheet）：汇总企业在某一时点拥有什么、欠着什么，并给出（用会计的眼光看）股东权益价值的估计。

利润表（income statement）：报告企业在分析期内赚了多少，同时提供收入与费用的明细。

现金流量表（statement of cash flows）：报告分析期内企业的现金流入与流出，并据此衡量现金收益（cash earnings，区别于会计利润）与现金流。

English originalThe Accounting Statements ! The balance sheet, which summarizes what a firm owns and owes at a point in time, as well as an estimate of what equity is worth (through accounting eyes). ! The income statement, which reports on how much a business earned in the period of analysis, while providing detail on revenues and expenses. ! The statement of cash flows, which reports on cash inflows and outflows to the firm during the period of analysis and allows for a measure of cash earnings (as opposed to accounting earnings) and cash flows.

幻灯 51. 资产负债表1. Balance Sheet

资产负债表把「资产」放在左边，把「负债与股东权益」放在右边。左边分四类：固定资产（长期有形资产）、流动资产（短期资产）、金融资产（证券投资等其他业务）、无形资产（非实物资产）；右边分四类：流动负债（短期义务）、长期债务、其他长期义务、股东权益。

English original1. Balance Sheet

幻灯 62. 利润表2. Income Statement

利润表是自上而下「做减法」做出来的：先有收入，每减掉一层费用，就得到一个口径不同的利润。所以「毛利高」与「净利高」讲的是完全不同的两件事——这正是分析师最常读错的地方。

幻灯 73. 现金流量表3. Statement of Cash Flows

现金流量表把企业的现金变动拆成三段：经营、投资、筹资，三段相加等于现金余额的净变动。

English original3. Statement of Cash Flows

幻灯 8三张表如何勾稽The Interconnections

三张表不是三份互不相干的记录，而是彼此咬合：利润表上的净利润会同时进入资产负债表（变成留存收益）和现金流量表（期初现金走到期末现金）；固定资产折旧既是利润表上的费用，也是资产负债表上资产账面价值的减少；经营、投资、筹资三类活动把资产负债表与现金流量表连起来。

English originalThe Interconnections

幻灯 9会计准则The Accounting Standards

会计是一套规则驱动的过程。随着时间推移，这些规则被逐步形式化——尤其针对上市公司。形式化有两个动因：

  • <strong>标准化</strong>：让公司之间可以比较。
  • <strong>第一性原理</strong>：确保利润、资产价值与现金流测的确实是它们该测的东西。

全球各地的准则仍不尽相同，但大体收敛到两套：

  • <strong>GAAP</strong>（Generally Accepted Accounting Principles，美国公认会计原则）：由 FASB（财务会计标准委员会）制定，用于美国财务报告。
  • <strong>IFRS</strong>（International Financial Reporting Standards，国际财务报告准则）：由 IASB（国际会计准则理事会）制定，供全球上市公司采用，截至 2020 年约有 90 个国家采用。
English originalThe Accounting Standards q Accounting is a rule-driven process, and over time, those rules have been formalized, especially for publicly traded companies. This formalization is driven by two considerations: q Standardization, to allow for comparisons across companies q First principles, to ensure that earnings, asset value and cash flows measure what they are supposed to measure. q While accounting standards around the world remain different, they have converged (for the most part) around two standards: q GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles), representing rules developed by FASB (Financial Accounting Standards Board) to cover US financial reporting. q IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards), representing rules developed by IASB (International Accounting Standards Board) for companies listed globally, followed by about 90 countries as of 2020.

幻灯 11小结The Bottom Line

我们做财务分析与估值的原材料，几乎总是以财务报表的形式出现。

因此，我们最好都理解会计人是怎么思考的（哪怕我们并不认同他们），才能看懂这些报表是怎么被拼起来的。

难点在于：随着时间推移，会计思维一直在变。我们必须理解这些变化——既包括变了什么，也包括为什么变——才能跟得上。

English originalThe Bottom Line ¨ The raw material that we use to do financial analysis and valuation almost always takes the form of accounting statements. ¨ Consequently, it behooves us all to understand how accountants think (even if we disagree with them) in putting these statements together. ¨ The challenge is that accounting thinking keeps changing, as we move through time, and we have to understand those changes (both the what and the why), to keep up with them.
