幻灯 2从绝对数到比率From Absolutes to Ratios

财务报表用绝对数计量经营成果——以美元、卢比或雷亚尔计，取决于记账币种。

绝对数很难、甚至不可能跨公司比较：在其他条件不变时，大公司本来就该有更高的利润、更多的负债。

比率把绝对值互相标定，于是可以：

  • 跨公司比较
  • 跨时间比较
  • 与基准比较
English originalFrom Absolutes to Ratios q Financial statements measure operations in absolute terms, i.e., in dollars, rupees or reais, depending upon the currency of denomination. q Absolute measures are difficult, if not impossible, to compare across companies, since bigger companies, all else held constant, should have higher dollar profits and carry more debt. q Ratios scale absolute values to each other, and allow for: q Comparisons across companies q Comparisons across time q Comparison to benchmarks

幻灯 31. 利润率1. Profit Margins

利润率有七八种口径，它们不是并列的，而是从利润表上一层层剥出来的：变动成本先退出得到贡献毛利，再退出全部营业成本得到毛利，再退出 SG&A 得到营业利润，往下才是利息、税与净利润。选哪个口径，取决于你要回答什么问题。

English original1. Profit Margins

幻灯 4贡献毛利率与毛利率：生产成本的口径Contribution & Gross Margins: The Costs of Production

贡献毛利率（contribution margin）衡量你每多卖一个单位赚到的「纯利润」——它只扣掉生产这个单位所对应的变动成本，因此很多软件公司的贡献毛利率接近 100%。

毛利率（gross margin）是它的近亲，既直接衡量边际盈利能力，也间接反映收入增长有多少能流进利润。作为例子：Zoom 是危机期间少数股价上涨的股票之一，它 2019 年的毛利率是 92%。

在其它条件不变时，贡献毛利率与毛利率都高的公司，利润潜力远大于毛利率低的公司。

English originalContribution & Gross Margins: The Costs of Production • Contribution margin measures the pure profits that you generate with every marginal unit you sell, since it nets out only the variable cost associated with producing that unit, giving many software companies close to 100% contribution margins. • Gross margins are a close relative, providing a direct measure of marginal profitability and an indirect measure of how revenue increases flow into profits. To illustrate, Zoom, one of the few stocks that has seen its value increase during the crisis, reported a gross margin of 92% in 2019. • Companies with high contribution and high gross margins have much more profit potential, other things remaining equal, than companies with low margins.

幻灯 5营业利润率：度量与含义Operating Margins: Measures and Implications

营业利润率衡量的是扣除其他营业费用之后还剩多少。这些费用无法直接对应到单个产品单位，但经营又离不开它们。

如果这些其他营业成本（比如 SG&A）相对生产成本是固定的（或更固定），那么毛利率与营业利润率之差就成了潜在规模经济（economies of scale）的一个简单代理指标。

毛利率高而营业利润率低的公司，在规模放大时，其营业利润（与利润率）的改善速度，应当远快于毛利率与营业利润率相近的公司。

English originalOperating Margins: Measures and Implications • Operating margins measure what is left after the other operating expenses of the company, which cannot be directly traced to individual unit sales, but are nevertheless necessary for its operations. • To the extent that these other operating costs (like SG&A) are fixed (or more fixed) than the costs of production, the difference between gross and operating margins becomes a simple proxy for potential economies of scale. • Companies with high gross margins and low operating margins should see operating profits (and margins) improve much faster as they scale up than companies where operating and gross margins are similar.

幻灯 6EBITDA 利润率：度量与含义EBITDA Margin: Measures and Implications

EBITDA 是经营现金流的粗略度量——粗略在于它还没扣税，也没扣资本开支。

尽管如此，它仍然是衡量公司现金创造能力的指标（在管理层自主决定再投资多少、借多少之前），并且被这样使用：

  • <strong>贷款人</strong>用它判断公司还借不借得起钱——因为债务是要在资本开支之前先还的；
  • <strong>股权投资者</strong>用它判断整个业务的估值是否合理（在它试图扩张资产基础之前）。

EBITDA 利润率高的公司，每一美元收入产生的现金流更多，因而应当比 EBITDA 利润率低的公司更能借到钱。

English originalEBITDA Margin: Measures and Implications ¨ The EBITDA is a rough measure of operating cash flows, rough because it is before taxes and capital expenditures. ¨ Notwithstanding that, it remains a measure of the cash generating capacity of a company, prior to discretionary choices (on how much to reinvest and borrow) and is used by ¤ Lenders to determine whether the company can afford to borrow money, since debt has to be paid before capital expenditures are made. ¤ Equity investors to decide whether the entire business is fairly valued, before it tries to expand its asset base. ¨ Companies with high EBITDA margins generate higher cash flows per dollar of revenues and should be able to borrow more than companies with lower EBITDA margins.

幻灯 7净利率：度量与含义Net Margins: Measures and Implications

扣掉税与利息费用，再把现金与交叉持股的收入加回来，得到净利率——它衡量的是每一美元收入里，有多少最终留给股权投资者。

这是一个混杂而嘈杂的指标：它同时反映公司的经营模式、税务负担、财务杠杆（债务带来利息费用，也影响税），以及非经营性资产。

净利率高的公司，总体能给股权投资者更多利润——但未必每股更多（如果净利率低于营业利润率的原因正是债务）。

English originalNet Margins: Measures and Implications ¨ Netting out taxes and interest expenses, and adding back income from cash and cross holdings, yields net margin, a measure of what equity investors get to keep out of every dollar of revenues. ¨ It is a mixed and noisy measure, reflecting a company's operating model, its tax liabilities and its financial leverage (since debt creates interest expenses and affects taxes), as well as non-operating assets. ¨ Companies with high net margins deliver more profits for equity investors, in the aggregate, but perhaps not a per share basis (if debt is the reason why net margins are lower than operating margins).

幻灯 8利润率的生命周期视角A Life Cycle View of Margins

同一比率在不同发展阶段含义完全不同。Damodaran 给出的对照表：初创期看毛利率（还在亏钱，SG&A 多是固定成本）；年轻成长期看毛利率或 EBITDA 利润率（基础设施与折旧是现金流变量）；高速成长期看营业利润率（规模效应会体现在更高的营业利润率上）；成熟成长期看税后营业利润率（NOL 用完之后税要真交）；成熟稳定期看净利率（债务结构稳定，为股权投资者创造回报）；衰退期看 EBITDA 利润率（用 EBITDA 还债、把现金还给股东）。

English originalA Life Cycle View of Margins

幻灯 92. 会计回报2. Accounting Returns

会计回报，是把利润按项目或业务的投入规模标定后得到的比率。

大体上，会计回报的度量方式有三处差异来源：

  • <strong>利润怎么算</strong>：只算给股权投资者的部分（净利润），还是算给债权人与股权投资者两方的部分；以及税前还是税后。多数情况下，会计回报以权责发生制利润为基础。
  • <strong>投入怎么算</strong>：只算股权投资者的投入，还是债权与股权投资者的总投入。多数情况下，会计回报用账面价值作为投入的度量基础。
  • 任何会计回报指标，因为<strong>时点</strong>不同（期初、期末或平均投入资本）都会得出不同的数值。

一致性规则：一个口径一致的会计回报指标，必须把利润和投入同时归到同一群人（股东或全部资本）身上。

English original2. Accounting Returns ¨ With accounting returns, profits are scaled to measures of investment in a project or business. ¨ Broadly speaking, there can be differences in how accounting returns are measured based upon ¤ How profits are measured, i.e., to just equity investors (net income) or to both debt and equity investors and whether profits are before or after taxes. In most cases, it is accrual income that is the basis for returns. ¤ How investment is measured, i.e., investment made just by equity investors or by debt and equity investors. In most cases, accounting returns use the book value as the basis of investment measurement. ¤ With any measure of accounting return, you can get different values depending upon timing, i.e., start of the period, end of the period or average for invested capital. ¨ Consistency rule: A consistent measure of accounting return will measure both profits and investment to the same group (equity or capital).

幻灯 10净资产收益率（ROE）Return on Equity

ROE 的分子有三种取法，分母有三种取法——组合起来就是九种 ROE。选哪一栏，取决于你在问什么问题。

English originalReturn on Equity

幻灯 11投入资本回报率（ROIC）Return on Invested Capital

ROIC 与 ROE 的差别在于：分子用经营利润（且要按前面几讲的做法做租赁与研发调整），分母用全部投入资本（账面权益 + 账面债务 − 现金及有价证券）。Damodaran 在这页把调整步骤全部写了出来。

English originalReturn on Invested Capital

幻灯 123. 效率比率3. Efficiency Ratios

效率比率衡量的是：公司把钱再投回自己的生意里，能换回多少收入。

其中周转率（以收入为分子）是用得最广的效率指标，但分母可以不同：

  • 营运资本周转率 = 销售额 / 非现金营运资本（或营运资本中的单项，如存货、应收款）
  • 资产周转率 = 销售额 / 总资产
  • 资本周转率 = 销售额 / 投入资本
English original3. Efficiency Ratios ¨ Efficiency ratios measure the revenue payoff that companies get from reinvesting back in their businesses. ¨ Turnover ratios, with revenues in the numerator are the most widely used measured of efficiency, though the denominator can vary: ¤ Working Capital Turnover = Sales/ Non-cash Working capital (or individual items of working capital, like inventory or receivables) ¤ Asset Turnover = Sales/ Total Assets ¤ Capital Turnover = Sales/ Invested Capital

幻灯 134. 度量财务杠杆4. Measuring Financial Leverage

债务比率衡量一家公司借了多少钱——相对于总资本，或相对于盈利/现金流。

债务既可以相对总资本，也可以只相对权益：

  • 债务资本比（Debt to Capital）= 债务 / (债务 + 权益)：衡量公司资本中有多少来自债务。
  • 产权比率（Debt to Equity）= 债务 / 权益：债务资本比的近亲，把债务表述为权益的百分比。

债务也可以相对盈利/现金流来衡量：

  • Debt to EBITDA = 债务 / EBITDA：衡量公司相对于其经营产生的现金（税前、资本开支前）背了多少债。
English original4. Measuring Financial Leverage ¨ Debt Ratios measure how much a company has borrowed, relative to overall capital or to earnings/cashflows. ¨ Debt can be scaled to overall capital or just to equity ¤ Debt to Capital = Debt/ (Debt + Equity): This is a measure of how much of the capital in a company comes from debt. ¤ Debt to Equity = Debt/Equity: This is a close variant of debt to capital, with debt stated as a percent of equity. ¨ Debt can also be measured relative to earnings/cashflows: ¤ Debt to EBITDA = Debt/EBITDA: This measures how much debt a company has relative to the cash it generates from operations, before taxes and capital expenditures.

幻灯 14算法上的变体Variants on calculation

债务算哪些：只有长期债务 / 全部计息债务 / 含承诺（如租赁）的债务。

账面还是市场：债务与权益用账面价值（取自资产负债表）；或用市场价值——上市公司权益用市值，债务市值若能取得也用市值。

总额还是净额：总债务（gross debt）是全部债务；净债务（net debt）是全部债务减去现金及有价证券。

English originalVariants on calculation ¨ What to include in debt ¤ Only long term debt ¤ All interest bearing debt ¤ Debt inclusive of commitments (like leases) ¨ Book or Market ¤ Book values for debt and equity (from balance sheet) ¤ Market values, measured as market cap for equity in a publicly traded firm, and if doable, market value of debt ¨ Gross or Net ¤ Gross debt is all debt ¤ Net debt is all debt minus cash & marketable securities

幻灯 155. 度量流动性与信用风险5. Measuring Liquidity/ Credit Risk

保障倍数（coverage ratios）：衡量公司履行承诺时有多少缓冲。

  • 利息保障倍数：承诺是利息费用，对标的是营业利润。
  • 固定费用保障倍数：承诺扩大到含债务本息，对标的是营业利润 + 固定费用。

流动性比率：衡量公司有多少流动资产可以应付近期需要与支出。

  • 流动比率（current ratio）：流动资产相对流动负债。
  • 速动比率（quick ratio）：只看流动性强的流动资产相对流动负债（存货通常剔除）。
English original5. Measuring Liquidity/ Credit Risk ¨ Coverage Ratios: These ratios measure how much buffer or coverage a company has in meeting commitments. ¤ With interest coverage ratio, the commitment is interest expenses, and it is scaled to operating income. ¤ With a fixed charge coverage ratio, the commitment is expanded to include debt payments, and it is scaled to operating income + fixed charges. ¨ Liquidity Ratios: These ratios measure how much liquidity companies have, to cover near-term needs or expenses: ¤ Current ratio, measure current assets relative to current liabilities. ¤ Quick ratio, looks at only liquid current assets relative to current liabilities. (Inventory is usually excluded.)

课后测试与答案Post-class test & solutions

题目与选项为中英对照；答案与解析附在每题下方。

第 1 题

如果你想衡量一家企业在规模扩大时提升利润的能力，应该看下列哪一个盈利能力指标？

If you wanted to measure the capacity of a business to increase profits as it scales up, which of the following measures of profitability would you look at?

  • a. 毛利率 Gross margins
  • b. 营业利润率 Operating margins
  • c. 净利率 Net margins
  • d. 毛利率与营业利润率之差 Difference between gross and operating margins
  • e. 营业利润率与净利率之差 Difference between operating and net margins
  • f. 毛利率与净利率之差 Difference between gross and net margins
答案：d 毛利率与营业利润率之差

在营业成本代表变动成本、其他营业费用相对固定的前提下，毛利率高而营业利润率低的公司，更能从规模经济中受益。

English originald. Difference between gross and operating margin. To the extent that COGS represents variable costs and other operating expenses are fixed, companies which have high gross margins and low operating margins should benefit more from economies of scale.

第 2 题

净资产收益率与投入资本回报率都是用来衡量公司投资质量的，两者计算时分母都用账面价值（权益与投入资本的账面值）。为什么用账面价值而不是市场价值？

The return on equity and the return on invested capital are designed to measure the quality of investments made by a company and both are computed using book value measures for equity and invested capital in the denominator. Why do we use book values (rather than market values) in this computation?

  • a. 因为账面价值比市场价值波动小 Because book values are less volatile than market values
  • b. 因为账面价值比市场价值更准确地反映资产的价值 Because book values more accurately reflect the values of assets than market values
  • c. 因为账面价值更可能反映已投入现有资产与项目的资本 Because book values are more likely to reflect the capital invested in existing assets and projects
  • d. 因为对多数公司来说，你可能拿不到权益的市场价值和/或债务的市场价值 Because you may not be able to get the market value of equity and/or the market value of debt for most companies
  • e. 以上都不对 None of the above
答案：c 因为账面价值更可能反映已投入现有资产与项目的资本

市场价值可能比账面价值更好地反映资产当前的价值，但会计回报要衡量的是「在既有投入上赚了多少」，而不是「它们今天值多少」。

English originalc. Because book values are more likely to reflect the capital invested in existing assets and projects. Market values may reflect current values for assets better than book values, but the objective in accounting return is to measure how much you make on existing investments, given how much you invested in them (not how much they are worth today).

第 3 题

如果你想要一个能作为公司违约风险指标的债务比率，会用下列哪一个？请说明理由。

If you wanted a debt ratio that would be an indicator of default risk in a company, which of the following would you use? Explain why.

  • a. 总债务 / (总债务 + 权益) Total Debt/ (Total Debt + Equity)
  • b. 长期债务 / (长期债务 + 权益) Long Term Debt/ (Long Term Debt + Equity)
  • c. 短期债务 / (短期债务 + 权益) Short Term Debt/ (Short Term Debt + Equity)
  • d. 总债务 / EBITDA Total Debt/ EBITDA
  • e. 长期债务 / EBITDA Long Term Debt/ EBITDA
  • f. 短期债务 / EBITDA Short Term Debt/ EBITDA
答案：d 总债务 / EBITDA

困境风险来自还不出债，而 EBITDA 就是可以用来还债的现金。债务相对于 EBITDA 很高（即便债务资本比很低）的公司，困境风险更大；而且总债务比长期债务更全面地反映欠款规模。

English originald. Total Debt/ EBITDA. Distress risk comes from the inability to make debt payments and EBITDA is the cash available to make these debt payments. A company that has a lot of debt relative to its EBITDA has more distress risk, even if it has low debt to capital ratios. And total debt is a more comprehensive measure of what is owed than long term debt.

第 4 题

如果要为一家盈利波动、债务上升的公司计算利息保障倍数，意图衡量它对抗违约的缓冲，你会用下列哪一个？

If you are computing an interest coverage ratio for a company with volatile earnings and rising debt, with the intent to measure its buffer against default, which of the following would you use?

  • a. 当年 EBIT / 当年利息费用 Current year’s EBIT/ Current year’s interest expense
  • b. 历史平均 EBIT / 当年利息费用 Average EBIT over time/ Current year’s interest expense
  • c. 历史平均 EBIT / 历史平均利息费用 Average EBIT over time/ Average interest expense over time
  • d. 当年 EBIT / 历史平均利息费用 Current year’s EBIT/ Average interest expense over time
答案：b 历史平均 EBIT / 当年利息费用

既然公司盈利波动，我会用平均 EBIT；但由于其债务负担一直在上升，我应当看当年的利息费用（它会高于过去的利息费用），这更能指示公司实际欠了多少。

English originalb. Average EBIT over time/ Current year’s interest expense. Since the company’s income is volatile, I would use the average EBIT. However, since its debt load has been increasing, I should be looking at the current year’s interest expense (since it will be higher than the interest expenses in the past) and is more indicative of what the company owes.

第 5 题

周转率衡量的是投资效率，但通常要结合营业利润率一起读。请把下列四家公司按投资效率从高到低排序：

Turnover ratios are measures of investment efficiency, but often have to be read in conjunction with operating margins. Rank the following from most efficient to least investment-efficient companies:

  • a. 销售额/资本高，营业利润率低 High sales to capital and low operating margins
  • b. 销售额/资本低，营业利润率低 Low sales to capital and low operating margins
  • c. 销售额/资本高，营业利润率高 High sales to capital and high operating margins
  • d. 销售额/资本低，营业利润率高 Low sales to capital and high operating margins
答案：c 最高效：销售额/资本高且营业利润率高；最低效：销售额/资本低且营业利润率低；其余两种居中

事实上，销售额/资本与营业利润率的乘积就是投入资本回报率，资本回报越高，意味着投资效率越高。

English originalRanking from most to least efficient: Most efficient: High sales to capital and high operating margins; Least efficient: Low sales to capital and low operating margins; The other two will fall in the middle. In fact, the product of sales to capital and operating margin is the return on invested capital, with higher returns on capital going with more efficient investing. Pre-tax ROIC = Sales/Invested Capital * EBIT/Sales
