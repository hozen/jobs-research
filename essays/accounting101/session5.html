一句话核心

当代会计规则在很大程度上是 20 世纪为制造业写的，而经济重心早已从制造转向科技与服务。Damodaran 挑出四处口径不一致：税、股权激励、租赁、研发——每一处都既影响利润表数字，也影响你该怎么读这些数字。

本讲导读Session guide

这一讲是全课程的转折点：Damodaran 列出四类「会计准则当年没跟上、后来又没改干净」的地方——税（三个税率互相打架）、股权激励、租赁、研发——然后逐个说清楚：会计怎么记的、报表会长什么样、以及他为什么认为记错了。租赁那一段他把话说得最重：「把租赁当成债，道理上完全站得住；不需要任何理由，历史上也从来没人这么主张过。」研发那一段更直接：会计准则用来要求费用化的两条理由，「一条都不成立」。

课程封面。本讲的副题是「Cleaning Up Accounting」——把当年没处理干净的地方重新算一遍。

English originalSESSION 5: CLEANING UP ACCOUNTING Accounting for Finance

幻灯 2会计人的角色（再回看一次）The Accountant’s Role

会计人喜欢秩序与一致——从他们热衷于写规则这件事就看得出来。

话虽如此，今天实践中的会计，绝大部分是 20 世纪为那个世纪占主导地位的制造业公司详细发展起来的。

随着经济重心从制造业转向科技与服务业、企业的财务行为随时间改变，会计人在四个关键问题上一直很挣扎：

  • <strong>税</strong>，以及企业为避税或推迟缴税采取的动作；
  • 以股权（stock）形式发放的<strong>管理层薪酬</strong>；
  • 具有合同性质、但<strong>并不算债务</strong>的承诺；
  • <strong>不是为了实物资产</strong>、而是为了长期收益做的投入。
English originalThe Accountant’s Role q Accountants like order and consistency, as can be seen in their propensity to write rules. q That said, much of accounting as practiced today was developed in detail in the 20th century for the manufacturing firms that dominated that century. q As the center of economic gravity has shifted from manufacturing to technology & service companies, and corporate financial behavior has changed over time, accountants have struggled with four key issues: q Taxes, and the actions that companies take to avoid or delay paying them. q Managerial compensation in the form of equity (stock) q Commitments that are contractual but are not debt q Investments for long term benefits that are not in physical assets

幻灯 31. 税：三个税率在打架1. Taxes: Dueling Tax Rates

① 边际税率（marginal tax rate）：法定税法条文里的税率。所以 2020 年一家美国公司就该按应税所得的 21% 在美国缴纳联邦税。由于美国公司现在按地区纳税的模式运作，跨国公司的边际税率会反映出它在哪里赚（或申报赚）应税所得。

② 实际税率（effective tax rate）：反映公司利润表上报告的税额与应税所得，而这套口径基于权责发生制：

  • 实际税率 = 税额 / 应税所得

③ 现金税率（cash tax rate）：反映公司就其应税所得实际付出的税款。

English original1. Taxes: Dueling Tax Rates 1. Marginal tax rate: The marginal tax rate is the tax rate in the statutory tax codes. Thus, in 2020, a US company should be paying 21% of its taxable income in the US as federal taxes. Since US companies now operate on a regional tax model, the marginal tax rate for multinationals will reflect where they make (or report to make) their taxable income. 2. Effective Tax Rate: The effective tax rate for a company reflects the taxes and taxable income it reports in its income statement, which is based on accrual accounting: ¤ Effective tax rate = Taxes/ Taxable Income 3. Cash Tax Rate: The cash tax rate for a company reflects the taxes it actually pays on its taxable income.

幻灯 4递延所得税资产与负债Deferred Tax Assets & Liabilities

对大多数公司来说，实际税率会低于边际税率，原因在于：

  • 在低税率国家有经营；
  • （合法的）税务递延与避税安排。

当账面（报告口径）与税务口径在「费用化了多少」与「申报了多少应税所得」之间出现差异时，由此产生的税款差异，在公司税簿上实缴税款少于（多于）财务报表所报告金额的情况下，列为递延所得税负债（资产）。

这么做的逻辑很简单：导致本期少缴（多缴）税的项目，将来会反转回来，届时就会多缴（少缴）税。

English originalDeferred Tax Assets & Liabilities q For most companies, the effective tax rate will be lower than the marginal tax rate, reflecting: q Operations in countries with lower tax rates q (Legal) Tax deferral and avoidance strategies q When there are differences between what is expensed and what is reported as taxable income between the reporting and tax books, the resulting difference in taxes is reported as a deferred tax liability (asset) if the company pays less (more) in taxes on its tax books than it reports in its financial statements. q The logic for doing so is simple. The items that give rise to less (more) taxes paid in the current period will reverse and result in more (less) taxes paid in future periods.

幻灯 5净经营亏损与结转Net Operating Losses & Carryforwards

美国公司亏损时，可以把这些亏损向后结转，用来抵减未来年份的应纳税额。

  • 在 2017 年税改法案（Tax Reform Act）之前，NOL 可以<strong>回溯两年</strong>，用来抵减过去年度已缴税款（相当于退税），并可向后结转 20 年。
  • 2017 年税改法案取消了回溯条款，允许亏损<strong>无限期向后结转</strong>。

当一家公司的亏损跨越多年，这些亏损会随时间累积成净经营亏损（Net Operating Loss, NOL），并应在公司财报中披露。

English originalNet Operating Losses & Carryforwards ¨ When a US company loses money, it is allowed to carry those losses forward and use them to reduce taxes paid in future years. ¤ Until the Tax Reform Act of 2017, the NOL could be carried back two years and used to reduce taxes paid in prior years (as a tax credit) and forward 20 years. ¤ The Tax Reform Act of 2017 removed the carry back provision and allows losses to be carried forward indefinitely. ¨ When a company has losses that are over multiple years, these losses are cumulated over time as a Net Operating Loss (NOL) and should be disclosed in a company’s financials.

幻灯 62. 股权激励是一项经营费用……2. Stock Based Compensation is an Operating Expense…

几十年来，公司用股权激励奖励员工，主要出于两个原因：

  • 让员工与管理层的利益与股东利益保持一致；
  • 弥补现金的缺失（凑出有竞争力的薪酬方案）。

股权激励主要有两种形式：

  • 以固定价格、在特定期限内购买公司股票（或投资其股权）的<strong>期权</strong>；
  • 公司的<strong>股份</strong>，有时附带交易限制（restricted shares，受限股）。
English original2. Stock Based Compensation is an Operating Expense… q Companies have used stock-based compensation to reward employees for decades for two reasons: q To align the interests of employees & managers with those of the shareholders q To make up for the absence of cash (to provide compensation packages that are competitive) q Stock-based compensation primarily takes two forms: q Options to buy the company’s stock (or invest in its equity) at a fixed price for a specified period. q Shares in the company, sometimes with restrictions on trading on those shares (restricted shares)

幻灯 7它到底算哪一类费用？What type of expense is it?

不管发放股权激励的动机是什么（利益一致，还是缺现金），它显然是一项薪酬费用。

  • 如果授予的规模大、偶发，而且主要是为了利益一致，那么有理由把它<strong>分摊到各年</strong>。
  • 如果公司持续使用股权激励，而且更多是为了弥补现金紧张而不是为了利益一致，那就是一项<strong>年度费用</strong>。

要把股权激励费用化，就必须在授予当时对给员工的期权或股票进行估值。

  • 2004 年之前，公司可以有一定裁量权，用授予时的行权价值来估算期权授予的价值。
  • 2004 年之后，FAS 123 要求公司用期权定价模型按<strong>时间价值</strong>给期权估值，并在授予当年确认这项费用。
English originalWhat type of expense is it? ¨ No matter what the motive for providing stock-based compensation (to align interests or to make up for lack of cash), it is clearly a compensation expense. ¤ If a grant is large and occasional, and primarily driven by the desire to align interests, there is an argument that it should be spread out over time. ¤ If a company uses stock-based compensation consistently, and more to make up for its cash poor status than to align interests, it is an annual expense. ¨ To expense stock-based compensation, you have to value of the options or stock given to employees, at the time they are granted. ¤ Until 2004, companies were allowed leeway to estimate the value of option grants based upon exercise value at the time of the grant. ¤ After 2004, FAS 123 requires companies to value options based upon their time premium (using option pricing models) and show that expense in the year the options are granted.

幻灯 8那它算不算现金流？Is it a cash flow?

既然期权和受限股现在被（正确地）当作薪酬，并在授予当年费用化，争论就转移到了另一个问题：它们是不是非现金费用（像折旧那样），因而应该在算现金流时加回来。

大多数公司和分析师似乎都认定答案是「是」——很多公司在算 adjusted earnings 时把股权激励加回来。

我强烈不同意。像折旧这种非现金费用（你一分钱没付）和「用股权（期权或股份）替代现金」之间，存在根本性的差别。

English originalIs it a cash flow? q Now that options and restricted stock are treated (correctly) as compensation, and expensed in the years they are granted, the debate has shifted to the question of whether they are non-cash expenses (like depreciation), deserving of being added back to get to cash flows. q Most companies and analysts seem to have come down on yes as the answer, and many companies add back stock-based compensation to get to adjusted earnings. q I disagree strongly. There is a fundamental difference between a non-cash expense like depreciation, where you pay nothing, and giving a share of equity (options or shares) in lieu of cash.

幻灯 93. 租赁就是债3. Leases are debt

债的本质：当你借钱，你制造的是对未来的合同义务，履行不了，你的持续经营（going concern）就会受威胁。

租赁合同：当你签下租赁合同，你同样制造了对未来的承诺，履行不了，你的生存同样受威胁。道理很简单，没有任何理由（而且从来就没有过理由）把租赁区别对待。

租赁的会计处理：2019 年之前，会计界存在分歧，把租赁分成两类：

  • <strong>资本租赁（capital leases）</strong>：承租方对资产拥有实质所有权，按债务处理，同时确认一项对应资产；
  • <strong>经营租赁（operating leases）</strong>：承租方在一段时间内（临时）使用该资产，按经营费用处理，资产负债表上既不列负债也不列对应资产。

从 2019 年起，GAAP 与 IFRS 都要求公司把所有租赁承诺一律按债务处理，无论交易如何设计。

English original3. Leases are debt ¨ The Essence of Debt: When you borrow money, you create contractual obligations for the future, and a failure to meet them can put your survival as a going concern at risk. ¨ Lease contracts: When you sign a lease contract, you create commitments for the future, and a failure to meet these commitments will put your survival at risk. Put simply, there is no reason (and there never has been) to treat leases as debt. ¨ Accounting for leases: Until 2019, accountants disagreed and broke leases down into two groups: ¤ Capital leases, where the lessee has effective ownership of the asset, is treated as debt, with a counter asset. ¤ Operating leases, where the lessee has (temporary) use of the asset for a period, were treated as operating expenses, with no debt or counter assets on the balance sheet. Starting in 2019, both GAAP and IFRS are requiring companies to treat all lease commitments as debt, no matter how structured.

幻灯 10把租赁资本化Capitalizing Leases

把租赁承诺转成债务的过程，与给任何银行债务或公司债定价的逻辑类似，步骤并不复杂：

  • 从未来各年的<strong>合同租赁承诺</strong>开始，按年份列明；
  • 根据公司的<strong>违约风险</strong>，估算它当前的<strong>税前借款成本</strong>；
  • 用税前债务成本作为折现率，计算租赁承诺的<strong>现值</strong>。

租赁承诺的现值按债务处理，并以相同金额列示为一项对应资产。

要把闭环合上，还需要对租赁债务计算利息费用、对对应资产计算折旧，并把它们放进利润表：

  • 租赁债务的利息费用 = 租赁承诺现值 × 税前债务成本
  • 租赁资产的折旧，按租赁期（视同资产寿命）与所选折旧方法计算。
English originalCapitalizing Leases ¨ The process of converting lease commitments to debt follows a simple process, akin to how any bank debt or corporate bond can be valued. ¨ Here are the steps: ¤ Start with the contractual lease commitments for future years, by year. ¤ Compute the pre-tax cost of borrowing for the firm today, based upon its default risk. ¤ Take the present value of lease commitments, using the pre-tax cost of debt as your discount rate. ¨ The present value of lease commitments is treated as debt, with the same value shown as a counter-asset. ¨ To complete the cycle, you compute interest expenses on the lease debt and depreciation on the counter asset and bring them into your income statement. ¤ Interest expense on lease debt = PV of lease commitments * Pre-tax cost of debt ¤ Depreciation on lease asset is computed using the life of the lease (as the life of the asset) and the depreciation method chosen.

幻灯 11其他合同承诺Other Contractual Commitments

会计（终于）接受把租赁当债务这件事了，但还有一大批合同承诺具备与租赁完全相同的特征，需要同样的处理。举几个例子：

  • 许多制造企业的<strong>采购承诺</strong>；
  • 流媒体公司（如 Netflix）的<strong>内容承诺</strong>；
  • 职业体育球队的<strong>球员合同</strong>。
English originalOther Contractual Commitments ¨ While accounting has (finally) come to terms with treating leases as debt, there are a whole host of contractual commitments that share the same characteristics as leases, and require the same treatment. ¨ Here are some examples: 1. Purchase commitments for many manufacturing firms 2. Content commitments at a streaming company (like Netflix) 3. Player contracts for a sports team

幻灯 124. 研发就是资本开支4. R&D is a cap ex

如果资本开支的本质是「这项费用的预期收益不只体现在本期，也体现在未来各期」，那么研发（R&D）费用显然完全符合。

话虽如此，全世界的会计准则大体上要求公司把研发费用化，用的是两条理由之一：

  • 研发的收益太不确定，所以在研发更接近商业化之前应当费用化；
  • 估算利润时保守一点总没错。

两条理由都站不住脚。

  • 不确定这根棍子，从来没有用在其他类型的资本开支上（比如建一家工厂去生产全新且未经验证的产品）。
  • 会计要给出的是<strong>最贴近现实</strong>的利润估计，不是最保守的估计。
English original4. R&D is a cap ex ¨ If the essence of a capital expenditure is that it is an expense whose expected benefits are not just in the current period, but in future periods, research and development (R&D) expenses clearly fit the bill. ¨ That said, accounting rules around the world, for the most part, require companies to expense R&D, using one of two rationale: ¤ The benefits of R&D are too uncertain. Consequently, they should be expensed until the R&D is closer to commercial development. ¤ It is better to be conservative in estimating earnings. ¨ Neither justification makes sense. ¤ Uncertainty is never used with other types of capital expenditure (building a factory to make a new and untested product) as the divining rod for capital vs operating expenses. ¤ Accounting should deliver the most realistic estimate of earnings, not the most conservative.

幻灯 13把研发资本化Capitalizing R&D

要把研发资本化，分三步：

  • <strong>第一步：</strong>为研发估计一个可摊销年限——凭你的最佳判断，研发平均需要多久才能回本（以商业化为标准）。
  • <strong>第二步：</strong>收集过去若干年的研发费用（往前回溯到可摊销年限的起点）。如果公司成立时间没这么久，就尽量多收几年。
  • <strong>第三步：</strong>对过去每一年研发，估计：① 今年要摊销掉多少；② 研发费用中还剩多少未摊销。

要把闭环合上，最后两步是：

  • 调整利润：把当期的研发费用加回来，再减去过去年份研发的摊销；
  • 把未摊销的研发作为资产列示，并以相同金额增加账面权益。
English originalCapitalizing R&D ¨ To capitalize R&D, there are three steps: ¤ Step 1: Estimate an amortizable life for R&D by making your best judgment on how long it takes, on average, for R&D to pay off (as commercial success). ¤ Step 2: Collect R&D expenses from past years (going back as long as the amortizable life). If your company has not been in existence for that long, collect as many years as you can. ¤ Step 3: For each of the past years of R&D, estimate n How much you will be amortizing this year n How much of the R&D expense remains unamortized ¨ To complete the cycle, here are the last steps: ¤ Adjust earnings by adding back the current year’s R&D expense and subtracting out the amortization of past years R&D ¤ Show the unamortized R&D as an asset, and show the same amount as an increase in book equity.

幻灯 14其他类资本开支Other Capital Investments

还有一些费用符合研发的特征——都是为了让收益在很多年里持续产生——但因为这些投入不是实物资产，就被当作经营费用处理。举几个例子：

  • 消费品公司为<strong>打造品牌</strong>而发生的广告费用；
  • 咨询公司为<strong>建立咨询业务</strong>而发生的招聘与培训费用；
  • 石油公司的<strong>勘探成本</strong>；
  • 订阅制或用户制公司的<strong>获客成本</strong>。
English originalOther Capital Investments ¨ There are other expenses that fit the R&D profile, i.e., expenses designed to create benefits over many years, but since these investments are not in physical assets, they are treated as operating expenses. ¨ Here are some examples: ¤ Advertising expenses by a consumer product company to build up brand name ¤ Recruiting and training expenses by a consulting firm to build its consulting practice ¤ Exploration costs for an oil company ¤ Customer acquisition costs for a subscriber or user based company.

幻灯 15小结：可以信，但得自己核The Bottom Line: Trust, but verify…

财务报表反映的不仅是企业对自身的「会计视角」，还背着会计史与历史遗留规则的包袱。

分析一家公司时，你应当从财务报表开始，但你完全不必忌讳对这些报表做改动、修正或重做——以便它们真正服务于你想用这些数据回答的问题。

English originalThe Bottom Line: Trust, but verify… ¨ Accounting statements reflect not only an “accounting” view of the company, but the burden of accounting history and legacy rules. ¨ When analyzing a company, you should start with accounting statements, but you should have no qualms about changing, modifying or redoing them to reflect what you are trying to do with the data in those statements.
