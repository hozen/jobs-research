一句话核心

现金流量表不只是「现金变了几多」的流水账：它同时告诉你公司真正赚了多少钱现金（cash earnings）、把钱投到了哪里、以及从股东和债主那里拿了多少又还了多少——而且全程站在股权投资者的角度看。

本讲导读Session guide

这一讲处理现金流量表。Damodaran 先把表的「终极目标」讲清楚：表面上是解释现金余额这一期变了多少、为什么变；里面却还嵌着三层信息——本期到底产生了多少现金收益（cash earnings，对照利润表里的会计收益 accrual earnings）、公司把多少现金再投资到哪里去维持和扩大经营、以及从债务与股权投资者那里筹集与返还了多少现金。整张表保留正负号，并且始终是从股权投资者的视角在看现金流。 然后按三段拆。经营段：间接法从净利润起步，加回折旧摊销与其他非现金费用，再按营运资本科目的变动加减——同样一个净利润，营运资本占用越多的公司，经营现金流越难看。投资段：投向经营性资产的钱（capex 与收购）是经营增长的引擎，用股票支付的收购不进这一段；投向非经营性资产的钱则形成另一条独立的价值来源，回报落在营业利润线以下。筹资段：还债本金属于这一段（利息才在经营段），发债与还债相抵可以互相抵消；分红与回购都让现金离开公司，也都减少股东权益，但回购对卖股与不卖股的股东效果完全不同。

课程封面页。副题仍是「Accounting for Finance」——这一讲的对象是现金流量表：现金从哪儿进来、到哪儿出去，以及为什么。

English originalSESSION 4: CASH FLOW STATEMENTS – CASH IN AND CASH OUT Accounting for Finance

幻灯 2现金流量表到底要解决什么问题The End Game with Cash Flows

现金流量表（statement of cash flows）在表面层面的目标，是解释一家公司的现金余额在一段期间里变了多少、以及为什么变。

但这张表里还嵌着另外一些信息：

  • 公司本期产生了多少现金收益（cash earnings），对照利润表里的会计收益（accrual earnings）；
  • 公司本期把多少现金、再投资到哪里，以维持并扩大经营；
  • 公司从债务投资者与股权投资者那里筹集了多少现金，又返还了多少现金。

现金流量表保留现金流的正负号：净流出记作减号，净流入记作加号。而且它始终是从股权投资者的视角在看这些现金流。

English originalThe End Game with Cash Flows q The surface level objective of a statement of cash flows is to explain how much the cash balance of a business changed during a period and why it changed. q Embedded in the statement of cash flows, though, is other information including: q How much cash earnings the company had during the period, as contrasted with accrual earnings (in income statements) q How much and where the company reinvested cash during the period to sustain and grow its business q How much cash it raised from or returned to its debt and equity investors q The statement of cash flows preserves the signs on cash flows, with negative cash flows shown as minuses and positive cash flows as pluses. It also looks at cash flows through the eyes of equity investors in the company.

幻灯 3重看现金流量表的结构Revisiting the Cash Flow Statement

把整张表摊开看：经营活动、投资活动、筹资活动三段净现金流相加，等于现金余额的净变动。三段各自装的东西并不一样——经营段装的是税后、付息后的经营净现金；投资段装的是资产的买与卖（资本开支、资产剥离、金融资产的买卖，以及收购别家公司付掉的现金）；筹资段装的是股权与债务的发行与偿还，以及分红。

English originalRevisiting the Cash Flow Statement

幻灯 41. 经营活动现金流1. Cash flows from Operations

经营现金流（cash flow from operations）用间接法构建：从净利润起步，加回折旧摊销与其他非现金费用，再按营运资本科目的变动（应收账款、存货、应付账款、应交税款等）逐项加减，最后落到经营现金流。

这张表就是整段经营现金流的「路线图」——起点、每一步加什么减什么、终点是什么。

English original1. Cash flows from Operations Change in non-cash working capital

幻灯 5营运资本的影响The Working Capital Effect?

经营现金流里嵌着营运资本科目的变动，但要注意把现金本身剔除掉：

  • <strong>非现金营运资本（non-cash working capital）＝ 非现金流动资产 － 无息流动负债（non-debt current liabilities）</strong>
  • 非现金营运资本增加 → 现金流减少；非现金营运资本减少 → 现金流增加。

在营运资本占用现金与资本的程度上说：在同样的净利润水平下，营运资本需求越高的公司，其经营现金流就越低。烧钱换增长的公司，净利润可以很漂亮，现金流却一路往下走——差别就在这里。

English originalThe Working Capital Effect? ¨ Embedded in the cash flow from operations is the change in working capital items, excluding cash ¤ Non-cash Working capital = Non-cash current assets – Non-debt current liabilities ¤ An increase in non-cash working capital will decrease cash flows, whereas a decrease in non-cash working capital will increase cash flows. ¨ To the extent that non-cash working capital ties up cash and capital, a firm with higher needs for that working capital will have lower cash flows from operations, for any given level of net income, than a firm with lower needs.

幻灯 62. 投资活动现金流2. Cash Flows from Investing

分节页：进入第二段——投资活动现金流。这一段讲的是公司把现金投到哪里去，重点是分清经营性资产与非经营性资产的投资。

English original2. Cash Flows from Investing

幻灯 7经营性资产，还是非经营性资产Operating or Non-operating Assets

投资活动一节同时包含对经营性资产和非经营性资产的投资，唯一的例外是对流动性强、几乎无风险的证券的投资——那部分被当作现金与有价证券（cash & marketable securities）处理。

投向经营性资产的投资，无论是内部的 capex（资本开支，扣除剥离）还是外部的收购其他公司，是驱动经营项目（收入、营业利润等）增长的引擎。注意：用股票支付的收购理所当然不会出现在这里。

投向非经营性资产的投资，则形成另一条独立的价值来源：它的回报不出现在经营项目里，而是落在营业利润线以下——体现为来自交叉持股或证券的收益（income from cross holdings or securities）。

English originalOperating or Non-operating Assets q The investing activities section includes investments in both operating and non-operating assets, except for investment in liquid, close to riskless securities, which is treated as cash & marketable securities. q The investments into operating assets, whether internal (cap ex, net of divestitures) or external (acquisitions of other companies) are the engine that drives growth in the operating line items (revenues, operating income etc.) Note that acquisitions funded with stock will not show up here for obvious reasons. q The investments into non-operating assets create a separate source of value, where the payoff will not show up in the operating line items but below the operating income line, as income from cross holdings or securities.

幻灯 83. 筹资活动现金流3. Cash flows from Financing

筹资段装的是公司与债务持有人、与股权投资者之间的资金往来：借进来是流入，还出去是流出；增发是流入，分红与回购是流出。两条线各自轧差，再加总就是筹资活动现金流。

English original3. Cash flows from Financing

幻灯 9债务的现金流Debt Cash Flows

利息支出出现在经营现金流一节（它压低净利润，并在递延税项里体现出来），但偿还债务本金属于筹资段。

如果这些偿债有一部分或全部由新发债务来覆盖，那么对现金流的净影响可以被抵消，甚至转为正。

因此：债务总额在某期上升，就表现为现金流入；债务总额下降，就表现为现金流出。那些着手在若干年内把债务压下来（或加上去）的公司，应当事先就预期到这些结果。

English originalDebt Cash Flows ¨ While interest expenses show up in the operating cash flow section, by reducing net income and showing up in deferred taxes, debt repayments are part of the financing section. ¨ To the extent that some or all of these debt repayments are funded with debt issuances, the net effect on cash flows can be neutralized or become positive. ¨ If total debt increases during a period, it will represent a cash inflow, and if it decreases, it will be a cash outflow. Companies that embark on plans to bring their debt down (up) over time should therefore expect these consequences.

幻灯 10分红与回购Dividends and Buybacks

直到 1980 年代，上市公司股权投资者收到的现金流只有股利这一种。分红的效果很简单：公司现金余额减少，每一位收到分红的股东口袋里现金增加。

从 1980 年代开始，美国公司以回购（buybacks）的形式向股东返还越来越多的资金：

  • 对公司而言，回购股票的效果与支付分红<strong>完全一样</strong>：现金离开公司。
  • 但对股东而言，现金流效果不一样：把股票卖回给公司的股东从公司拿到现金；没卖的股东一分钱没拿到，但他在公司里剩下的股权份额变大了。
  • 分红与回购都会减少资产负债表上的股东权益。
English originalDividends and Buybacks q Until the 1980s, the only cash flow that was received by equity investors in publicly traded companies was dividends. The effect of paying dividends is simple: it reduces the cash balance at the company and increases the cash in the pockets of every shareholder who receives dividends. q Starting in the 1980s, US companies have returned increasing amounts to their shareholders in the form of buybacks. q The effect of buying back stock is exactly the same as paying dividends, to the company, with cash leaving the company. q For shareholders, though, the cash flow effect is disparate. Those shareholders who sell their shares back get cash from the company, and those that do not get no cash, but get a larger share of the equity left in the company. q Both dividends and buybacks reduce shareholder equity on the balance sheet.

幻灯 11可供分红的现金（自由现金流给股权）Potential Dividends (Free CF to Equity)

分节页：本页只留了一个标题——「Potential Dividends（Free CF to Equity）」，即公司到底有多少现金可以分给股东，对应自由现金流给股权（free cash flow to equity）。课件在此处未给出正文。

课后测试与答案Post-class test & solutions

题目与选项为中英对照；答案与解析附在每题下方。

第 1 题

要算出经营现金流（operating cash flows），起步时我们该做下面哪一件事？

To get to operating cash flows, which of the following do we do, to start the process?

  • a. 从 EBITDA 起步，减去折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用 Start with EBITDA and subtract out depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges
  • b. 从 EBIT 起步，把折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用加回来 Start with EBIT and add back depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges
  • c. 从净利润（Net Income）起步，把折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用加回来 Start with Net Income and add back depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges
  • d. 从 EBIT 起步，减去折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用 Start with EBIT and subtract out depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges
  • e. 从净利润起步，减去折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用 Start with Net Income and subtract out depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges
答案：c c. 从净利润起步，把折旧、摊销和其他非现金费用加回来。

现金流量表是从股权投资者的视角编制的，所以它从净利润起步，而不是从 EBITDA 或营业利润起步。

English originalc. Start with Net Income and add back depreciation, amortization and other non-cash charges. The statement of cashflows is constructed from the perspective of equity investors and it starts with net income, not EBITDA or operating income.

第 2 题

接下来我们把流动资产（如存货）和流动负债（如应付账款）的变动带进来。下列哪一项最好地描述了这些变动的影响？

Following up, we then bring in changes in current assets, like inventory, and current liabilities, like accounts payable. Which of the following best describes the effect of those changes?

  • a. 存货增加会减少现金流，应付账款增加会增加现金流。 Increases in inventory decrease cash flows and increases in accounts payable increase cash flows.
  • b. 存货增加会增加现金流，应付账款增加会减少现金流。 Increases in inventory increase cash flows and increases in accounts payable decrease cash flows.
  • c. 存货增加会减少现金流，应付账款增加也会减少现金流。 Increases in inventory decrease cash flows and increases in accounts payable also decrease cash flows.
  • d. 存货增加会增加现金流，应付账款增加也会增加现金流。 Increases in inventory increase cash flows and increases in accounts payable also increase cash flows.
  • e. 存货增加与应付账款增加都不影响现金流。 Increases in inventory and increases in accounts payable do not affect cash flows.
答案：a a. 存货增加会减少现金流，应付账款增加会增加现金流。

存货增加会占用现金；而更多地动用供应商信用（应付账款），则释放现金。

English originala. Increases in inventory decrease cash flows and increases in accounts payable increase cash flows. An increase in inventory ties up cash flows, but an increased use of supplier credit (accounts payable) releases cash flows.

第 3 题

投资活动现金流里嵌着公司所做的收购。这些收购可以用现金支付、用股票支付，也可以用现金加股票混合支付。下列哪一项会出现在投资活动之下？

Embedded in investing cash flows are acquisitions made by the company. These acquisitions can be paid for with cash, with stock or with a mix of cash and stock. Which of the following will show up under investing activities?

  • a. 只有以股票支付的收购 Only stock-based acquisitions
  • b. 只有以现金支付的收购。 Only cash-based acquisitions.
  • c. 以现金支付的收购，以及混合支付收购中的现金部分 Cash-based acquisitions and cash portion of mixed acquisitions
  • d. 以现金支付的收购，以及混合支付收购中的股票部分 Cash-based acquisitions and stock portion of mixed acquisitions
  • e. 所有收购 All acquisitions
答案：c c. 以现金支付的收购，以及混合支付收购中的现金部分。

这是现金流量表，所以只有花在收购上的现金——全额或部分——才会出现在投资活动之下。

English originalc. Cash-based acquisitions and cash portion of mixed acquisitions. It is a statement of cash flows. So, only cash spent on acquisitions, in part or in full, show up under investing.

第 4 题

股利可以是现金股利，也可以是股票股利；股票回购可以是留作库存股（treasury stock），也可以是为了减少股本。下列哪一项会出现在筹资活动一节？

Dividends can be cash dividends or stock dividends and share buybacks can be either to keep as treasury stock or to reduce share count. Which of the following will show up in the financing section?

  • a. 所有股利，不含股票回购 All dividends, no share buybacks
  • b. 所有股利，所有回购 All dividends, all buybacks
  • c. 现金股利，不含股票回购 Cash dividends, no share buybacks
  • d. 现金股利，所有股票回购 Cash dividends, all share buybacks
  • e. 现金股利，仅限减少股本的那部分股票回购 Cash dividends, only share buybacks that reduce share count
  • f. 现金股利，仅限于留作库存股的那部分股票回购 Cash dividends, only share buybacks for treasury stock
答案：d d. 现金股利，所有股票回购。

股票股利没有现金效应；而股票回购，无论动机是什么，都是现金流出。

English originald. Cash dividends, all share buybacks. Stock dividends have no cash effect and all stock buybacks, no matter what the motive, are going to be cash outflows.

第 5 题

如果你在看一家非常年轻、高增长公司的现金流量表，下列哪一项是你会预期看到的？

If you are looking at the statement of cash flows for a very young, high-growth company, which of the following would you expect to see?

  • a. 经营活动现金流为正，投资活动现金流为负，筹资活动现金流为负 Positive cash flow from operations, Negative cash flow from investing and Negative cash flow from financing
  • b. 经营活动现金流为负，投资活动现金流为负，筹资活动现金流为正 Negative cash flow from operations, Negative cash flow from investing and Positive cash flow from financing
  • c. 经营活动现金流为负，投资活动现金流为正，筹资活动现金流为负 Negative cash flow from operations, Positive cash flow from investing and Negative cash flow from financing
  • d. 经营活动、投资活动、筹资活动的现金流全为负 Negative cash flow from operations, Negative cash flow from investing and Negative cash flow from financing
  • e. 经营活动、投资活动、筹资活动的现金流全为正 Positive cash flow from operations, Positive cash flow from investing and Positive cash flow from financing
答案：b b. 经营活动现金流为负，投资活动现金流为负，筹资活动现金流为正。

年轻的公司通常在亏钱，可加回的折旧也很少，同时又有很大的投资需求（为了兑现未来的增长）。这些现金流出都必须靠筹资活动注入的现金来补（主要来自股权融资）。

English originalb. Negative cash flow from operations, Negative cash flow from investing and Positive cash flow from financing. Young companies generally lose money and have little depreciation to add back, as well as substantial investment needs (to deliver future growth). These cash outflows all have to be funded with cash infusions from financing (mostly from equity).
